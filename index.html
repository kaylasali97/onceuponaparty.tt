A Little Magic, Thoughtfully Planned.

Every celebration deserves to feel special. At Once Upon A Party Co., we help parents turn their ideas into joyful, memorable experiences through thoughtful planning, creative details and trusted event suppliers.

Whether you're dreaming of a sweet little birthday gathering or a full themed celebration, we help bring the pieces together so you can spend less time worrying about the details and more time enjoying the moment.

We provide planning and coordination, working with independent suppliers to bring each celebration together.

How We Make Celebrations Happen

Children's Party Planning

Help with developing the concept, choosing a theme, organising details and coordinating the various elements of a celebration.

Party Coordination

Coordination of suppliers, deliveries, schedules, setup arrangements and event-day details, according to the agreed scope of service.

Themed Party Experiences

Thoughtfully coordinated celebrations inspired by children's favourite colours, characters, interests and imaginative themes.

Decorations & Party Styling

Sourcing and coordinating independent decorators, balloon artists, backdrops, rentals and other styling elements to suit the chosen concept.

Entertainment & Activities

Helping families source suitable entertainers, games, creative activities, arts and crafts, sensory play and other age-appropriate experiences.

Cakes, Treats & Catering

Coordination with independent cake makers, dessert vendors and catering providers to complement the celebration.

Party Favours & Personalised Details

Assistance with sourcing party favours, personalised items, invitations, activity sheets and thoughtful finishing touches.

Custom Celebrations

Bespoke planning and supplier coordination for families with a specific idea or a celebration that needs a more personalised approach.

Every celebration is different. Services can be tailored to your vision, requirements and event needs.

About the Founder

Hello, I'm the founder of Once Upon A Party Co.

My love for creativity, meaningful experiences and the little details that make a moment memorable inspired me to create a space where children's celebrations can be thoughtfully planned and beautifully brought together.

With a background in Early Childhood Care and Education, I appreciate how important play, imagination and age-appropriate experiences are in a child's world. I bring that perspective into the celebrations I help coordinate, with a special appreciation for creative activities, thoughtful details and experiences that children can genuinely enjoy.

My goal is to help parents bring their ideas to life with a little more ease, a little more creativity and a lot of heart.

Because childhood is full of little milestones worth celebrating — and those memories deserve to be special.
